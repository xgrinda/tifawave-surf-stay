# Editing the conversion homepage

Use **Website content → Page → home**, separately in English and French. The
existing draft, publish, private preview and revision workflow is unchanged.

- **Title / Introduction:** hero headline and supporting text. Defaults are
  “Come for the Waves. Stay for the Vibes.” and its French translation.
- **Hero image / description / video:** use real Tifawave media-library URLs.
  Keep a static photo even with a video. Mobile, data-saving and reduced-motion
  visitors receive the photo first; video is never required to request a stay.
- **Main text:** optional additional brand introduction, below the experience
  heading. Keep it short.
- **Questions and answers:** homepage FAQ accordion. If empty, published FAQ-page
  questions are reused, followed by truthful built-in defaults when none exist.
- **Search appearance:** homepage metadata in each language.

The twelve existing **Page sections** slots edit these headings and descriptions
in order. Empty heading/body values use the verified defaults; maintain slot
positions (add empty preceding slots when editing a later one).

**Packages:** while no genuine offers are published, **Page → packages**, first
three section headings/bodies, edits the three custom-enquiry cards. Their labels
remain clear that dates, accommodation, pricing and inclusions need a personal
quote. The selected enquiry topic pre-fills an editable message. Original starter
offers stay unpublished. Once real offers are entered/published through **Surf
packages**, up to three appear with source prices, durations and inclusions; their
IDs carry into the existing booking form. Supply French offer content as well.

**Rooms:** prices/photos come from the existing room catalogue; titles and
summaries can use **Room** documents in each language. Six listings are shown as
five categories, with the balcony-double variant within Double. Standard/twin
€35, balcony/triple €45, family €55 per room/night; dorm €15/person/bed/night.
Breakfast inclusion follows the business setting. Do not promise unverified
Wi-Fi, bathrooms, transfers or other amenities in copy.

**Lifestyle media:** the existing active gallery's food/breakfast category and
view/sunset category supply the real breakfast and atmosphere photographs. Room
photographs come from their listings. Update these through the existing media,
gallery and room controls; no stock or generated property imagery was added.

**Guest trust:** only active reviews with an HTTPS source link are shown, with
their own rating/date and source. No aggregate property rating is invented.
Without source-backed reviews, the section explains the real manual request
process. Publish only genuine, verified review text through Reviews.

**Protected booking facts:** a request is Pending, does not hold a room/bed, and
has no online payment or live availability guarantee. The Owner checks both OTAs
and separately managed reservations before approving. Keep this meaning intact
when editing. Additional meals, yoga, excursions and surf coaching are not
automatically included in accommodation.

**Conversion measurement:** fixed homepage CTA/intent/language codes and successful
pending-request/enquiry events use the existing production-only analytics setup.
They exclude names, contact details, messages, exact dates, guest counts, booking
IDs and URL queries. Preview does not send events to the production analytics
property. Real reporting requires the approved production measurement setup.
